9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundJuly 20, 2026
3rd String-Pin Champ Crowned

Such a great weekend for bowling, especially it being our 'Best 3 of 4' format. While we had mutliple bowling Tournaments up north and the FIFA World Cup Championship match, we had a great turn out of 162 entries for our Bowlero-Acme Tournament. We were able to payout a little over $11K with a perfect Cut-Line of 706. Our third event with the String-Pins again confirmed for us how perfectly they play for a 9-Tap format. The Cash List included FOUR 800's and a very diverse range of handicaps.

The 9-Tap Jackpot has continued to show its armor. Earl Kleist was close to knocking down the Jackpot but an errant 1st Frame cost him that chance and then missing 7-Middle Taps thanks to an 8th frame strike. That left the door open for Rick Kelley and Chris Hale to string the Front-5, each taking home $250.00. The Jackpot continues to grow as it will be $11,645.00 for our next event.

Crowning our 3rd String-pin Champion and our 14th 1st-Time Champion this year, David Suom came out on the lanes swinging. Only joining the 9-Tap Tour in February, he has continued to learn the game he picked up just a few months before he joined. From practice in Secoma and learning ball reaction and surface, he has even taken the time to learn how to drill his own equipment. Talk about someone invested. He said that he doesn't know what happened, but he shot his biggest game on Tour in his first game, then topped it in his third game. Shooting his highest series by a BUNCH with an awesome 872, definately showing that his practice has paid off. His previous high of 14th Place is now overshadowed by his earning the Blue Champion Jersey. He wants to thank Johnny Seng and Tina Tran for all the support and getting him into the 9-Tap Tour. Taking home a nice $2,390.00 he doesn't know what he will do it but is looking forward to adding more stars to his shirts.

Jay Pannette was the top bowler for majority of the day, shooting an outstanding 823 that ended up good enough for 2nd place. Not a stranger to the 9-Tap Tour, he was looking forward to taking home the Red Champion Jersey, but understands the competitve field the Tour offers. His $1,061.00 check bumps his lifetime cash to over $12K.

Even with a couple unfortunate splits throughout the entry, Willie Manning Jr showed his veteran status by throwing a great 808 series for 3rd place. Bringing home $546.00 he is just one championship away from breaking the lifetime cash milestone of $40k.

Next week we will be at Bowlero Lanes (Lakewood). Looking forward to seeing everyone there.
