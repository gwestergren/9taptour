Tour RECORD 928 Series!!!
NOTE: Squad Times for Westside Lanes

Had a great turn-out up north at Kenmore Lanes this weekend with a nice 181 entries. We paid out $12,887.00 to our bowlers with a slightly higher Cut-Line of 711. We had some very high scores thrown with SIX 800s shot throughout the day, including a three way tie at 803. As we had expected for Kenmore, our bowlers brought the competition and definitely pushed one another for amazing scores this weekend.

As predicted, Kenmore Lanes was going to crack the 9-Tap Jackpot. Kahlee Barrio and Jim Barrio were on the hunt, Tied at the Front-4, Jim missed on the fifth frame however Kahlee kept stringing the 9-Taps... stretching it to the Front-6, giving her a "Golden Shot" worth $9,316.00... the 7-pin teasingly stood for just a second but fell, giving her a real strike on the 7th shot. She still took home a nice $750.00 for her Front-6.

The first time the 9-Tap Jackpot paid out over $30,000.00 was in 2025, when it set the record at $35,935.00. Today we surpassed $30K for the 2nd time in 25 years at $30,030.00. With 6 Tourneys remaining, can we set a new record? The 9-Tap Jackpot took a slight hit to $11,614.00 for next weekend. Great Job Everyone.

Must be something about Kenmore Lanes but scores have consistently been high recently. Needless to say Steve Nolan came with his usual calm demeanor and shot lights out. Previously winning his first Title at Kenmore last June. His goal was to defend his previous Title and earn himself the Red Championship Jersey. With his mindset of "just roll the ball," he was able to shoot a new Tour RECORD for a 3-game series with a superhuman 928. The Tour usually sees one of these superscores once every 5 years or so. Steven's previous personal best was an 886, also shot in Kenmore when he won his 1st Championship. He wants to thank Vickie Adkins for their support this weekend. His $2,363.00 1st Place prize money will be going towards bills and the last 6 Tournaments of the year. Outstanding Job Steve!!!

Li'i Kalima-Marion has been on fire lately. Stringing three phenomenal games for an 846 series, he continued putting the pressure on other bowlers and pushing himself to the top of the leaderboard. He takes home a nice $1,050.00 for 2nd Place but is still vying for his 3rd Tour Championship, which we're sure is on the horizon. Awesome Performance!

Jesse Nagler also had a great performance this weekend. With his highest previous finish at 5th place, Jesse shot back-to-back great games for a personal series best of 823. He finishes in 3rd Place and earns a sweet check for $549.00. Great Job!

Next week we will be bowling our Tour favorite "Best 3 of 4" format at Westside Lanes. Looking forward to seeing everyone there.
