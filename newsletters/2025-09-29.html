HI Joy Bowl continued its string of big turnouts, although we just missed reaching 200 entries, with a total of 197 producing another great payout total of $12,670.00. As we mentioned in Wednesday's Newsletter, as we expected, Hi Joy's payouts have boosted us to over $500,000.00 for the year (now at $508,446.00), which puts us on pace to SHATTER our 2024 record ($561,897.00) and to even exceed $600,000.00 by the end of the year!!!

Talk about having a major assault on the 9-Tap Jackpot... it started in the very first game of the day when Andy Snow started with the Front-5, soon after a second Front-5, then later a third, all 3 getting a shot at #6 and $750.00. There were also a few more bowlers taking shots at the Middle-7. I can't remember getting called to video so many bowlers taking shots for $750.00. When all the smoke cleared, it was Justin Miller who stood alone as the only bowler to convert on the $750.00 shot and for good measure, he added an 8th Tap to the string.

Our Hi Joy Champion Austin Forrest has been a Tour Member since Feb. 2023, never experiencing a day like he had on Sunday. Prior to 2025, his best finish had been 27th Place. He hugely improved on that finish two weeks ago, when he finished in 3rd Place at Bowlero. Strangely, Austin said that his league average is down this year, while his Tour average has increased by SIX pins in his last two Tourneys. On Sunday, he got into that famous 'Zone', where EVERYTHING worked for him, shooting a monster 890 and winning by a huge 77 pins. When asked where that series came from, he had no explanation. When I asked if he had a new ball that was working for him, he said no, but he will be getting a new ball with some of his $2,194.00 in winnings. He used his Roto Grip 'Exotic Gem' ball, drilled by Randy at the Bowlero (Lakewood) Pro Shop, while wearing his Dexter SST8 shoes. Ironically, Austin wasn't going to enter the last squad. He shot well enough in the first squad to have a nice check coming to him, but he struggled in the second squad. After deciding to wait for his check, he figured he might as well bowl one more... boy did that pay off well!!! CONGRATS Austin on Win #1.

In 2nd Place, Osvaldo Gonzales also improved on his best previous finish with the only other 800 series (813) shot Sunday. He had two 4th Place finishes earlier this year, climbing the ladder a few more rungs toward his first Tour Title. A great $988.00 check should help tide him over until that first Win!!

In 3rd Place, Earl Kleist shook off a bit of a recent slump to put together a super 786 set. This week he didn't get a piece of the Jackpot pie, but instead he captured a piece of the Scratch Pot instead, earning an extra $160.00. His $524.00 in prize money, pushes his #2 All-Time career earnings to an enviable $85,050.00.
