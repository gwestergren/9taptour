Front 6-Taps = 'Golden Shot'
3rd-Time Champ Crowned

Bowlero-Paradise never fails to bring out the best in our bowlers and the sheer number that turned out for the 'Best 3 of 4' format and Mother's Day Pot was amazing. We had an outstanding 263 bowlers with a HUGE 55 bowlers taking home some money. It was great to see an appropriate TEN of those Cashers being MOMS. We arrived to the bowling center with some news of a glitch with the lane set-up. With that news, we expected to have some higher than average scores, which came true with the slightly higher Cut-Line at 736.

We also had our Mother's Day Side Pot, which produced a RECORD size Pot, so we paid 4 spots this year instead of the previous years 3 spots. The Top Four handicap games earned a sweet $450.00 for our Moms. Congrats to our Top Mom Kathy Matthews for rolling a phenomenal 329 taking home $200.00. Runner ups were Michelle Galdo $125.00, Maria Stuckey $75.00 and Ashley Kochel $50.00 all within 14 pins of each other. Great bowling from everyone.

Bowlero-Paradise was again the scene for another bowler taking a crack at our 9-Tap Jackpot. Vickie Victorio got that coveted 'GOLDEN SHOT', throwing ONE BALL for nearly $6,000.00 in cash. Unfortunately, a split on shot #7 prevented her from cracking the whole Jackpot. She did take home $500.00 for her Front-6 Taps.

Alex Cady took a minute to figure out which game he was going to get rid of during all three squads he bowled. Finally during the third squad he was able to get into his zone and shot a great 858. Not a stranger to the first place position as this will be Alex's 3rd Tour win and earning him the White Champion Jersey from Bowlifi. Alex is another bowler who has worked hard on his game, improving his average by an impressive 38 pins in the last 18 months. A special shoutout to Jeremy Lanouette for pushing him towards the win. Alex used both his Motiv Venom Hysteria & his Venom Shock drilled at Secoma Lanes for his big win. Taking home a nice $2,423.00 pushed him into the $10K level in career earnings, now at $11,800.00. Already looking forward to the next win at the Second Quarter Championship, so everyone be ready.

Coming in 2nd Place, shooting a remarkable 856 was Bob Anderson. Bob found his line during a re-entry, when he put several great games together. His final game surpassed his previous personal best by 22 pins, finishing like a Champ, but just TWO PINS short of the Top Spot! He took home a very nice $1,084.00 for his effort.

We had a tie for 3rd Place between Michelle Galdo & Brett Harrison. Both taking advantage of the higher scoring Saturday, scoring a whopping 848 series. Michelle (new to the Tour and Mark's sister) shot her personal best game by 16 pins on a re-entry, breaking the mark she had just set on her first entry. No stranger to the Tour, Brett was also vying for his first Champion Shirt. Look out everyone Michelle and Brett are coming for that Top Spot.

Congratulations to all our Winners and remember that we have a "BYE" next week. Looking forward to seeing everyone at Secoma for our Second Quarter Championship. Happy Mother's Day!!!
