Middle-7 Hit
Back to Back Tournaments!!!

Such a phenomenal turn out at Bowlero Lanes this weekend. We had 217 entires, which paid out $13,913. Scores were a little on the low side with a Cut-Line of 688 and no bowlers topping 800. The top 4 spots were separated by only 11 pins, that equates to a spare. Talk about some tough bowling.

As we had predicted, the 9-Tap Jackpot was going be HIT this weekend. For majority of the day the highest consolation was the Front-3 with 7 bowlers waiting to get paid. However, James Black and Samson Iballe had a chance for the Middle-7 but a real strike stopped their chance. Earl Kleist also missed a chance for a Middle-7 due to a split in the 8th frame. He ended with 8 of 9 taps with no payout.

Marilyn Mateo was able to string the Middle-7 earning her the top consolation of the day and a nice $750.00. If you don't remember, she broke the Jackpot with the Front-7 Taps on the opening day of our current season. Congratulations!!! The 9-Tap Jackpot grew slightly to $11,208.00 for next weekend. Great Job Everyone.

No stranger to the Champion's Circle, Samson Ibale took advantage of the moderately low scores and was able to secure the top spot by shooting an adjusted 790 series, squeezing in by 9 pins. This becomes his 37th Champion Win adding to his already impressive resume. He said he will continue to wear his 'Purple' championship shirt as its become his lucky shirting, also having worn it during his last win in February of 2026. He takes home a nice $2,581.00 and increases his all time cash to over $130K. Congratulations!!!

Rick Kelley was in the zone this weekend. Finding his line and being consistent throughout the day, he shot an amazing 781 series that was the bench mark for everyone to overcome. He also hit his highest scratch game on Tour of a 273. Having just joined us in June this has become his highest seed to date. He takes home a nice $1,142.00 for second place. Great job!

Marilyn Mateo made strong showing trying to earn her third championship. Just a spare away from the top spot she still took home the top consolation and a 3rd place check for $570.00.
