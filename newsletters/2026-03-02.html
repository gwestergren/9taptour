GREAT Scoring on Acme's
'String-Pins' on Sunday
Ladies Take 1st & 2nd

In just the Tour's 2nd 'String-Pin' event at Bowlero-Acme, the numbers couldn't have been more PERFECT in every measure we use! Balanced Cash List of ALL 'skill levels'... Ideal range of scores... a nice turnout of 185 bowlers producing a Cash List of 38 and Ladies taking the Top 2 Places. If you break down averages into 3 groups, what we had on Acme's Cash List was 13 High avg bowlers, 13 Middle Avg & 12 Low avg. Can't get any better balance than that. We were very pleased that the small oil-pattern adjustment we made for Sunday's 'Best 3 of 4' format worked out so well. The scoring couldn't have been more perfect, with 3 bowlers in the low 800's and a Cut-Line at an ideal 701, which means that every Member who cracked 700 earned a check.

The quest to string EIGHT 9-Taps for the 9-Tap Jackpot actually happened when Andy Snow strung the needed EIGHT straight Taps, but unfortunately it was the last 8 frames instead of the Front-8. Since 7-Middle Taps wasn't in effect Sunday, that left the Jackpot Consolation to go to Mike Alagar who hit the Front-5 Taps for a nice $250.00 payday.

Ruta Hert became the SEVENTH 1st Time Champion in just our 9th event of 2026. Ruta first joined the Tour in 2023, usually bowling with her 2-Time Champion husband Matt. After getting lined-up earlier, Ruta caught fire on a re-entry, posting her 2nd & 3rd best games ever, with both games coming in the same entry she SMASHED her previous Personal Best series. By finishing with a big game, Ruta seemingly came out of nowhere to capture the Title by 13 pins. When asked about her success secret, she said that she's been working to improve her bowling and that work has certainly paid off! We asked her what she would do with the $2,460.00 Winners share; her answer was quick & easy, all she said was, "NINE KIDS". She covered both of the Tour's equipment sponsors by using her STORM Summit ball drilled by Randy at Papa's Pro Shop (Bowlero) and also wore her Dexter SST 6s for the Win. Ruta wanted to thank her lane mates, hubby Matt, Andrew Fuller, Zac Clute & Jason Hummell.

A VERY HOT Stephanie Lanouette finished in 2nd Place for the second straight week. Last week she was 23 pins short of her 1st Title, at Bowlero-Acme she cut that margin to just 13 pins. Based on that progression, she should come within 3 pins next week... right? The $1,089.00 she earned hopefully soothed the sting of her near miss and also coming so close to her secret goal... getting that first Title before brother Jeremy does it. Great streak Stephanie!

In 3rd Place, Andy Snow had a GREAT day. He offset the bad timing of stringing EIGHT Middle 9-Taps when it didn't pay, by shooting a 300 in that same game, which combined with his scratch 975 series (4-games) to take a sweet $385.00 from the Scratch Pot. That went nicely with the $579.00 he took home for 3rd. The veteran Tour Member moved up to #14 on the Career Earnings list, currently at $32,013.00... which is not counting the TWO 9-Tap Jackpots he's hit, along with substantial other winnings from side-pots.
