Another 1st Time Champ -
SEVENTH IN-A-ROW

What another great turn out at West Seattle Bowl this weekend. 10 new Members joined the Tour and one of the new bowlers, Jesse Nagler, even made the Cash List. We had a total of 200 entries and a great payout total at $13,523.00. Scores ranged from moderate to slightly low throughout the day, with a 'Cut Line' of just 686.

Well, the 9-Tap Jackpot proved to be a challenge for everyone this week. We had Blake Lord-Witting and Keo Sai sharing the Consolation of the Front 4-Taps earning $50 each. Tina Tran had a great chance at hitting the Middle 7-Taps for $500.00 but "fell short on #7." Next week the Jackpot makes a HUGE jump to $5,100.00.

The 'Year of the Rookie' continues... For the seventh time in-a-row and TEN out of the first TWELVE 2026 events, we have crowned a First-Time Champion. Rob 'Robbie' Hess joined the Tour in 2025 and has tremendously improved his game throughout the year. Having not cashed in any previous event, he hit the Top Spot shooting the only 800 of the day and earning both his first Prize Money and Champion Shirt all-in-one. He would like to give a shout out to Brandon at WSB Pro Shop for drilling his Storm Hy-Road and his bowling lane mates. Rob took home a nice check for $2,590.00, which is pretty sweet for his first Tour payday! Congratulations Rob.

Returning to the Tour recently, after recovering from an injury, Blake Lord-Witting took 2nd Place earning $1,141.00. He found his groove and knocked out an impressive 799 to secure his 2nd Place standing, only 9 pins short of his second Tour Title. Great shooting and happy you are back bowling on the Tour again.

Rounding out the Top 3 was Charles Daughery Jr. shooting a great 787, earning him a nice $591.00 to put in his pocket. Overcoming a wrist injury, he is still pushing towards grabbing that Champion shirt. Great Job Charles.
