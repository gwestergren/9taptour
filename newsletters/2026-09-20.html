Three Time Champion Crowned!!!

What a wonderful sunny weekend we had at Bowlero-Paradise for our "Best 3 of 4" Event. We had a phenomenal 207 entries paying out over $14K. As we are accustomed to at Bowlero-Paradise, the Cut-Line was 733 and we had a whopping 9 800s thrown this weekend. Such great scores were shot and a huge congratulations to Jeremy Adolphsen for shooting his first 9-Tap 300 earning him his 300 patch. HUGE shoutout to Rain City Bowling Supply for donating shammies to our event today. Always appreciate your support to our bowlers and thank you!!!

The 9-Tap Jackpot was hit slightly this weekend with the Front-4 by SIX bowlers. Congratulations to Jim Barrio, Paul Kelly, Jeremy Adolphsen, Montana Xaysana, Jesser Sherman, and Earl Kleist. Each took home $100.00 for the consolation while the 9-Tap Jackpot grows to $11,002.00 for Secoma Lanes after the break. Great Job Everyone.

Paul Kelly was on "FIRE" at our event this weekend. Throwing out his lowest game he was able to find his line on both pairs of lanes and shoot back to back 270s, tallying a fantastic 866 series to earn his third championship. He is definitely thankful for the 9-Tap Tour and all its members for the competition. His 1st place check of $2,910.00 bumps his lifetime cash to over $15K. Congratulations!!!

Bob Anderson was one spare away from taking the top spot and ended up 5 pins shy. He was consistent throughout the day and shot the second highest series of the day with an 861. He will be taking home $1,274.00 which was also enough to hit the $10K lifetime cash milestone. Great shooting!!

Paul Mann shot a very nice 849 series securing his third place by a well placed spare. He will be taking home $649.00 for his performance.

Reminder that we will be on a "BYE" next week and will have our next event at Secoma Lanes on October 4th. Looking forward to seeing everyone there.
