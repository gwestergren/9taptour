9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundDecember 8, 2025
2 Hall of Fame Nominees
Claim Top Spots

This week the 9-Tap Tour traveled to Bowlero-Lynnwood. Thank you to all who made the trek in the nasty wind and rain to join us. This was the last chance for everyone to earn their invite to next week's Year-End Championship. Two Hall of Fame nominees showed why they made the ballot by finishing 1st and 2nd. There were many who were trying for their first title amongst the top finishers. After the Top 3 finishers, 5 of the next 6 were looking for that 1st Title.

For the second week in-a-row the 9-Tap Jackpot, continued to hold bowlers at bay. Last week 5 bowlers couldn't get past the Front-3 Taps. This being the season of sharing/giving, Freddie DeGuzman, Adam Steen, Steve Nolan, Sol Villena, Samson Ibale & Marshall Carter all stuck together, only getting the Front-3 earning them $75.00 each.

Chuck Hufford shot the ONLY 800 of the Tourney, his 807 was enough to claim career Title #9, earning him $1,050.00. ALL 9 of his Titles were won with $0 in the Progressive Pot... STILL he's now reached an impressive Career Total of $29,955.00 in Prize Money. Chuck is one of 5 Hall of Fame Nominees for 2025, the results of the Member Voting will be announced Sunday at the Year-End Championship. Chuck's first Tour Title came back in 2008, while his most recent came 13 months ago.

The second Hall of Fame nominee, Greg Burdsall finished 2nd Sunday, shooting 779 and claiming a hefty $1,150.00. His career earnings are also similar to Chuck's now at a nice $29,507.00. Greg was locked in at B-Lynnwood, also taking 1st place for both the Scratch High Game & Series this week, collecting an extra $382.00 from the Pot.

Shooting 768 and rounding out the Top 3 was Dean Hale. Dean brought his year long drought to an end a couple weeks ago finishing 21st at Paradise. His 3rd place finish this week earned him a sweet $608.00 payday.

We hope to see everyone next week at Bowlero Lanes (Lakewood) for the Year-End Championship & Awards which will be given out prior to the Tournament. So, even if you didn't earn a spot in the event, you're welcome to come out and cheer on the rest of the "family" and celebrate all the award winners, as well as the chance to say Farewell to Linda who is retiring after 20+ years of running the Bracket Table. Wishing everyone a GREAT Holiday Season. Rob, Shellie, Karen & Mark.
