An eventful & exciting day at Kenmore Lanes Sunday! One of the day's highlights came in the first squad, when Skip Shear nearly tossed his first 'sanctioned' 300 game. He ended up with 11 real strikes, but one 9-count in the 10th cost him his milestone. If Skip had gotten that one extra pin, it would have been the ELEVENTH time the Tour hosted a Perfect Game and the 300 Ring that goes with it.

In the 2nd squad, Rob Blaisdell strung 6 out of 7-Middle Taps, leaving the 9-Tap Jackpot Bonus to go to Cal Fisher who had the Front-4 Taps, earning him the $100.00 Consolation.

Then in the 3rd Squad we watched a dramatic finish by a Tour Veteran, breaking out of his slump (see below). Kenmore's historically aggressive lanes did prove difficult for many, but overall the scores were perfectly ideal with TWO bowlers reaching 800 and a 'Cut-Line' that was right on target at 695, so every bowler that reached 700 made it onto the Cash List, just like we hoped to see.

Samson Ibale broke out of his slump Sunday that saw him only making the Cash List ONCE since mid-October. Thanks to that slump, he had racked up 4 Bonus Pins, which offset his -2 pins of handicap. He clearly didn't need the extra pins, winning by a hefty cushion of 44 pins. We have a pretty good idea of how he escaped his slump. He just got home last week from a nice vacation in the Philippines. He not only got to relax and unwind during his trip... he also bowled in a few Tournaments while he was there. Samson made a strong showing in both events, as the Top Qualifier in one, ultimately finishing 4th, then adding another 4th Place finish in the other. Heading into the 3rd squad, Samson was on a re-entry and he was already lined up... so the low Top score on the board made for a great target. Entering his final game, he still needed a scratch 257 game to pull out the Win. Like a true Champion, he stepped up and threw a 300 to win it in style. Now in his 25th year on the Tour, SIX of the 'lefty's' 36 wins have been at Kenmore Lanes. Samson wanted to thank his good friend James Black who supported him and bowled with him all day.

Skip Shear Jr almost had his 6th Career Title Sunday. A 2nd Place check worth a sweet $988.00 pushed his Prize Money earnings to an impressive $26,945.00. Where Samson has a history of Wins at Kenmore, Skip has a similar history, winning 3 of his 5 Titles at Secoma Lanes.

Alex Cady is at the other end of the spectrum from Samson. His 3rd Place finish Sunday continues his recent 'Hot Streak'. During the streak, he's Cashed EIGHT times in the last NINE Tournaments, also including a 2nd Place finish.
