Air Force Champion Crowned!!!
20th Veteran's Day Tournament

What a SPECTACULAR showing for our 20th Veteran's Day Tournament at Bowlero-Paradise Lanes this weekend. With an INCREDIBLE 241 entries, we paid out $21,298.00 to our bowlers including a RECORD $2,900.00 to our Veterans in the "Vets Pot." The lanes were challenging with a Cut-Line of 672 and only one 800 shot.

Grateful to have so many of our military Veterans participating in the "Vets Pot" this weekend, which we expanded to payout to the Top EIGHT handicap games. Top honors went to Joel Brown (AF) with a huge 324 game. Branch 'Bragging Rights' went to the Navy last year, after Army dominated the year before... this year, we had an odd THREE-WAY TIE. Army, Navy & Air Force each had TWO bowlers that made the Top 8, since Dale Roberts spent most of his 17 years with the Navy, after starting in the Army.

With the support of our sponsors, we had over $2,100.00 in various donations to give out to our bowlers. Storm started us off by donating three bowling balls. Congratulations to Alyssa Wight, Dale Jones, and Tina Tran for winning a ball of their choice. Most notable was our Golden Ticket from Les Schwab Tires that was awarded to Leroy Cofield worth 40% off a full set of new tires. American Lake Golf Course provided a Golf Package worth around $300.00 with the lucky winner being Veteran Chuck DeForest, who also won the same package last year.

The 9-Tap Jackpot took the high-ground this weekend. Earl Kleist had a chance for the Middle-7 but was derailed by a split. The highest consolation for the day was the Front-4, shot by our Joyce Roberts, Bill Sprinkle, and Andrey Williams. They each took home $150.00. Congratulations to you all. The 9-Tap Jackpot increases to $12,234.00 for next weekend.

Joel Brown was on fire this weekend, stringing three GREAT games for an 843 series. This also included his 324 game that earned him the top veterans handicap game. He said he was a little worried because of the number of great bowlers that were at Bowlero-Paradise but pushed through it and focused on his game. He wants to thank Susan Jimerson and Aron Golden for their support and pushing him to bowl better. His $2,340.00 1st Place prize money pushes his lifetime cash past the $10K milestone and earns him his 2nd Tour Championship and Red Jersey. Outstanding Job Joel and Thank You for your Service!!!

Samson Ibale continues to expand his Bowler of the Year points. Shooting a nice 769 series he secured his second place finish by one spare. This is the second year in a row he placed 2nd in the Veteran's Tournament. He takes home a nice $1,046.00. Awesome Performance!

Aaron San Luis, had a great set of games this weekend, including a 285 and 282 toward the Veterans Handicap Pot for a nice $740.00 payout. His 760 series was nine pins shy of taking the 2nd place spot but earned him a nice check for $558.00. Great Job and Thank you for you Service!
