Tower Lanes & 9-Tap Tour
15 Year Reunion
Top 3 All Aiming for Title #2

Wow, what a great reunion Saturday at Tower Lanes. Bob Hanson and crew welcomed the Tour in true fashion. All squads were full to capacity and ran without any hiccups.

A message from Bob Hanson, Owner: "We appreciate everyone coming out and making today such a success. Watching everyone having a great time was amazing. We look forward to a continued relationship with the Tour and its Members."

What lane condition to use was a guessing game this week and looks like we hit it right on the nose. With the exception of one extreme score, the Cash List was perfectly within our 'Goldilocks' zone. There were a lot of single high games tossed all day, but many didn't seem to have the company they needed to make for a good series. After 7 straight 1st-Time Champs, this week the Top 3 finishers were all vying for career Title #2.

The Tour grew again this week with a few of our new family members shooting some pretty big games. Don't forget to bring your family and friends to join our wonderful family and earn your Life Member status.

Taking Title #2, Manuel Mason breaks the streak of 1st-Time Champions, shooting an insane 884 in the 1st squad, shattering his Personal Best. Manny's been steadily improving his game this year, which has put him at or near the Top of the current Bowler of the Year standings. Earning an awesome check for $2,328.00, Manny plans to use his winnings toward his upcoming trip to Vegas. With his fiancée Imelda & friends there to support him, they helped him to keep his nerves in check. Stating that he felt he was "On a roll and feeling really comfortable", propelled him to his record Win. He reentered anyway, but couldn't quite find the same groove again.

An 884 was definitely a hard score to beat. But, Andy Darr took a strong shot at it, tossing an impressive 827. He recently captured Title #1 and this week was one spot short of Title #2. A sweet 2nd Place check for $1,036.00 still made for a profitable day anyway.

Rounding out the Top 3, also earning his first Title recently, was Dale Roberts Jr. Shooting a great 817 series wasn't quite enough this week, although that score would have been enough to Win in a few Tourneys this year. Taking home $544.00 was a nice reward for his effort.

Thank you Bob and the entire staff of Tower Lanes for the hospitality. Looking forward to our next visit soon.
