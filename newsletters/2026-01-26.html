HUGE Scorefest @ All-Star
Kenmore Next Sunday

Well the inaugural visit to All Star Lanes was a success despite the cold temperatures and the West Sound and South Sound City Tournaments going on and of course the main event of the day, the Seahawks Game. A few lucky Tour Members were at the Seahawks game, many of those that couldn't attend the game in person showed their support at the Tourney. What a GREAT Seahawk win and it was nice watching the game together!

Seems like every 3 or 4 years we have a slip-up with the wrong oil-pattern (house shot) being put out on the lanes. When that happens, we always seem to have fun with all the BIG scores that result. To avoid having those high scores impact our bowlers average, we won't count any of the scores for average purposes. The Top 5 finishers this week were all chasing Title #1. The 9-Tap Jackpot got off fairly easy, with Teddy Drake being the lone bowler to reach the Front-5 Taps, giving her $250.00 extra Pocket Cash for the week. So, the Pot grows for next week at Kenmore, which also keeps the 7-Middle Taps in play as well.

After chasing a Title for 3 1/2 years, we're excited to see Dale Roberts Jr capture his FIRST Tour Championship. Dale joined the Tour in May 2022 and has been close with some Top 10 finishes. Dale used his STORM Ion Max (he won on the Tour) to shoot his MONSTER 895, out-distancing TWENTY-THREE other 800's shot on the House-Shot Sunday. This Win earned Dale a sweet $2,690.00, making his 1st Win even more memorable. He bowls numerous leagues in the West Sound Association and he was certainly excited that we were at All Star, one of his home Centers. Dale would like to thank Johnny Seng, Tina Tran, Red & Marilyn Mateo for all their support and the 9-Tap Tour for being a big happy family.

2026 is turning out to be a season of ties for the top of the Cash List. This week we had a tie for 2nd Place thus sharing 2nd & 3rd Place. Dwayne Davis & Manuel Mason both shooting enormous 885s to finish just one mark shy of their first Titles. They each took home an impressive $900.00 for the day. Following right behind them was Bobby Braxton & Collin Dionne with a 4th Place tie, with another BIG score of 868.
