Year of First Time Champs

Wow what a weekend! Although attendance was lower than expected, we still had a healthy 246 entries. Scores were pretty low after three squads on Saturday, with Bien Garcia the only bowler over 800, shooting an 801. During the final squad to qualify we had a tie between Ashley Kochel and Jeremy Price both shooting 805 to take over the weekend's Top Qualifier spot. A 9th & 10th roll-off earned Jeremy the #1 Seed and the TQ Jacket. Bien did not go home empty handed though, his 801 did earn him the new STORM ball as Saturday's Top Qualifier. Congratulations to Bien and Jeremy!

Forty-Nine Members advanced to the finals where we saw some awesome tight matches and some big scores. Top 13 Qualifiers earned themselves a 'BYE' during the first Round. From here the cuts began, with the bottom 16 going out after round 1, working our way to the Sweet 16 finalists. When all was said and done we crowned our NINTH First-Time Champion already this year (6th in-a-row).

Our 1st Quarter Champion, Stephanie Lanouette worked hard to make it to the finals. Shooting 705 in the final squad, she was able to make it to the Top 49. It was a lot of work from there. She decided to take it one game at a time and by the end she was so tired, but with her friends and family cheering her on, she was able to pull it together and win the Championship Match against Andrey Williams, which she admitted to being very nervous. The Win was all the more satisfying after coming up just short with TWO previous 2nd Place finishes. Stephanie earned a great $2,910.00 for all her hard work. She plans to use some of her winnings for her trip to Vegas to compete in the Women's Championship and like any Mom/Grandmother would do, spoil her kids and grandkids. She would like to send a special thank you out to Amy and Charles for their support during qualifying and a HUGE thanks to her inspiration and brother, Jeremy Lanouette, who she PROUDLY beat to the Winner's Circle!

Well, the 9-Tap Jackpot proved to be a challenge for everyone again this week only allowing the Front 4 by Derrick Callao & Marilyn Mateo each earning $50. That left the Pot to make another HUGE jump to $4,405.00. Passing $4K automatically triggers the return of 7-MIDDLE TAPS, for the upcoming West Seattle Tourney.

In 2nd Place, Andrey Williams was also chasing his first Tour Title. I suspect it won't be too long for the first Win, considering how much he's improved his game. The $1,280.00 2nd Place check should help soothe the wounds of his near miss.

Our two 3rd Place bowlers Ashley Kochel & Yanis Green each had great runs all the way to the Final 4. Both were winning head-to-head matches by sizeable margins, with some really nice games, up until the Final 4. Each took home $470.00 for their efforts.
