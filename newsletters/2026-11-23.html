1st-Time Champion!!!

What a FANTASTIC Tournament this weekend at Angel of the Winds. Had 157 entries make the trip paying out a very nice $11,912.00. Scores were absolutely phenomenal with a PERFECT Cut-Line of 705 and just two 800s shot. Definitely had numerous BIG games thrown all day by a wide range of bowlers, led by three 300's with Greg Burdsall shooting TWO of them. Even our Top 3 finishers followed the casino theme with 7 7-7 for handicaps. Each of them had a different variation of 7 pins, +7 -7 +7(5+2), they all hit the Tourney JACKPOT!!! We also had the largest invasion of our Canadian family members ever.

The 9-Tap Jackpot stood strong this weekend. Troy Breeding, Charles Clarke, & Leo Morzo each had great looks toward the race for the coveted Front-7 but all tapped out after the Front-4 & tied the highest Consolation for the Tournament. They each took home $150.00 for their efforts. The 9-Tap Jackpot grows slightly to a solid $12,451.00 for next weekend, just a couple hundred dollars shy of the 9-Tap Tour record. Great Job Everyone.

Starting their 6+ hour trip from Campbell River (on Vancouver Island) heading for Angel of the Winds at 3:00AM on Sunday, Miles Nerona was determined to make his long trip worth it. With the support of his wife Amy, he was able to focus, despite just one hour of sleep, making his approach to each shot look effortless. The Neronas run the Bowling Center in Campbell River (CR Bowling), contributing to his late start after a busy Saturday night. Finding a line that worked Sunday, he was able to string three great games toward a great 841 series, leading to his first Tour Championship. Previously he placed 2nd in 2024 and more recently took 3rd at our last 3rd Quarter Championship. It's safe to say that this win was well worth the wait. He wants to thank his lovely wife Amy for all her support as well as all of his fellow Tour members from Canada that came down to bowl. He will be using his 1st place check of $2,678.00 to take the family on a trip and future Tour events. Phenomenal Job!!!

Samson Ibale finds himself again in 2nd Place for the second Tournament in-a-row. If only he wore his lucky purple bowling shirt he might have won. His 803 series continues to earn him BOY points, trying to lock in this year's Bowler of the Year Title. He takes home a nice $1,176.00 for 2nd Place. Awesome Performance!

Greg Burdsall made an impressive run towards the leaderboard. Shooting a 300 game in two straight squads, he was able to string enough high games for an awesome 790 series. Hopefully next time he will string all of his 300s in one squad. His 3rd Place check for $603.00 pushes his lifetime cash to over $30K. Great Job!!

BIG DOINGS next week we will be at Lucky Strike-Lynnwood for a 'Best 3 of 4' format. This will also be our last weekend to earn Bowler of the Year points and all the bowlers Sunday will be voting for the Tour's 2026 Hall of Fame inductee. Watch for Wednesday's Newsletter for details.
