9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundAugust 4, 2025
Over $12,000.00 Paid Out
183 Bowlers = 37 Paid Out

This Sunday, 9 Tap Tour went back to West Seattle Bowl for the second time this year after a long hiatus. With 183 entries, we found 37 bowlers on the cash list. Scores were near the Goldilocks zone as the cut line was a little high at 729, but a perfect 4 bowlers hitting the 800+ mark.

The 9-Tap Jackpot was not seriously threatened throughout the day, but still had some drama. After the 2nd squad, Thang Duc Thai was on the leaderboard with the Front-4 Taps. His brother, Huyen Thai, decided to chase him down in final squad and take it away from his brother hitting the Front-5 and getting a real strike in the 6th frame to stop the run. Huyen earned the $375.00 consolation prize for his efforts, and the 9-Tap Jackpot is now at $9,319.00 for next week's tournament.

There are people that believe in the 'Power of Positive Thinking'. Rob was handed a note by one of our bowlers, telling him not to open it until the end. The sender was Brandon "BP" Parker, and he predicted his FIRST Tour Win. Scoring a great 830, he did just that!! Brandon used his brand new Storm Physix drilled by Robert Kelley at Secoma Lanes. Brandon said he is going do something nice with his family, and invest the rest in his company BP Brands, with his $2,415.00 check. Congratulations Brandon!

Doing his best to chase down this week's champion was a past champion Kalym Henry. Kalym was near perfect in all 3 of his games, but a split in the 8th frame of the final game made him come in just short with a score of 824. Kalym earned a $1,115.00 check plus another $220.00 out of the scratch pot for an excellent performance.

Taking 3rd place this week, Clemencia (Cheneil) Lopez shot her best series ever with an 812. This is Cheneil's first Top-3 finish, and with her great shooting earned herself a $588.00 check.

Make sure you open up your Wednesday newsletter for next week's Special Tournament info, as we help Secoma Lanes Celebrate long-time owner Mike Martin. We will have some special drawings giving away some Storm donated loot.
