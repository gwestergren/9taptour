Hi Joy Crowns Double Champion!

Wow - what an exciting day at Hi Joy. Lanes proved to be a little tough with only two bowlers shooting 800. As with many of the other events, 2026 seems to be the year for sharing. We had 10 ties, one being a 4-way.

Well, one bowler didn't share this week. Manuel Mason shot 847 to claim his first Tour title and earning him $2,276.00. But that wasn't enough, he also hit the 9-Tap Jackpot with the front 7 earning him an additional $4,835.00.

Since joining the Tour in May 2025, Manuel has made it on the cash list quite a few times, even taking 2nd place a couple times. He had lots of support Saturday from his tour family. He says he was a little nervous finishing up knowing he was close to a title score, but the fact he had already hit the 9-Tap Jackpot also brought lots of excitement. Manuel plans to use the funds to help with an upcoming move and for a little fun on his trip to Vegas in June.

Shooting 824 and claiming 2nd place was Stephanie Lanouette. She was definitely locked in and gave her all trying to claim her first title. Her efforts earned her $1,020.00. Watch out she's on a roll and definitely someone to watch for.

This week we had 15 new bowlers join the Tour family. One, Alec Nelson came in hot and ready to bowl. Alec rounds out our top 3 finishers this week with a 776 earning him $397.00. Welcome Alec and congratulations to you and all our cashers this week.
