9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundDecember 15, 2025
Spectacular 2025
Year-End Championship
& Awards Ceremony

Incredible to believe we just concluded the final event of the 9-Tap Tour 2025 Season at Bowlero Lanes. An awesome field weathered the storm and rising waters to attend the culminating event, with total payouts of $12,499.00 including 100% of the Winner's Pot worth $1,820.00.

Prior to the start of practice we held the end of the year awards ceremony. Acknowledging the Top 10 Bowler of the Year recipients with a plaque and their choice of a new bowling ball, DEXTER Shoes, or STORM bowling bag. BIG SHOUT-OUT to STORM for their continued support and RAIN CITY PRO SHOP who was the primary Sponsor Sunday.

The World Cup format Sunday was both fun and challenging, producing two extra (17th & 18th) places on the Cash List. Jared Burdine & Brian Peters took home $155.00 each, just missing spots in the Sweet 16. The YEC Top Qualifier, with the only 800 shot in Qualifying (808), was Ty Richardson. Ty earned one of FIVE new Storm balls presented on Sunday and also one of the new Top Qualifier Jackets.

Sweet 16: Ty Richardson 253 d. Brian Ward 175, Kris Tong 275 d. Ruong Tang 176, Austin Forrest 222 d. Brian Selck 219, Jeremy Lanouette 260 d. Chandra Martinez 194, Brandon Parker 258 d. Matthew Hert 255, Lii Kalima 232 d. Calvin Jones 171, Stephanie Perrine 218 d. Walt Dalrymple 183, Kalym Henry 242 d. Marily Mateo 219.

The final match between Kalym Henry and Jeremy Lanouette was a great finals to watch. While the lanes had transitioned, each of them were doing everything they could do to make the adjustment. Early 8 counts and splits made it difficult for Jeremy to string enough strikes to push his way to his first tournament win. Kalym had a few messengers that kept him on top, earning him his THIRD career Title and the YEC Pennant that will hang in our Centers before events. Jeremy took home a nice $1,095.00 for his second place finish. He will definitely be a contender next year. Congratulations Kalym for your 1st place win and taking home $2,195.00.

Kalym Henry has been bowling for his College team, maintaining an average in the 190s. The experience he's gained was certainly showing, as he maintained his focus throughout the Finals. Our sponsors were well represented by Kalym as he wore his Dexter SST-8s and threw his Storm 'Pitch Black', drilled at the Somoff Shop at Secoma. He asked us to pass his THANKS to his Dad (Bill) for being there all day to support him.

We want to express our THANKS to all our Members for your continued support and another great Record Breaking season. We will be on a two week BYE with our next season starting January 3rd at Bowlero-Paradise, with a Best 3 of 4 Format with squads at 10:00, 12:30, and 3:00. Happy Holidays from Rob, Shellie, Karen & Mark.

Sunday was also a Celebration of Linda's retirement after 23 years of running the Bracket Table. The Members and Staff present each had the opportunity to leave a message in her 'Memories Book' and say their goodbyes. ALL OUR BEST WISHES LINDA!
