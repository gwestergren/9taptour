9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundAugust 3, 2026
Two time Champion &
'Daily Double' Success!!!

Such a phenomenal weekend for bowling. A total of 347 entries generated a payout of $28,173.00 over the course of two days (two separate tournaments) for our 'Daily Double' format. We could not have done this without the dedication and competitive nature of our members, to include the wonderful staff at Secoma Lanes and Jeff Swanson. Sunday was no different from the success we had on Saturday. The longer pattern produced on 'par' scores across the field with a PERFECT 692 cut line and only seperated the top three spots by FIVE pins. Truly shows the grit our bowlers have on any pattern thrown at them.

The 9-Tap Jackpot has shown its durability once again this weekend. We had quite a few chances for the the Middle-7, with Jason Laye, Andy Snow, and new Member Gloria Schoenwald all missing on the seventh ball. Instead, FIVE bowlers tied with the Front-3 Taps (a repeat from Saturday), earning $75.00 each ($375.00 total paid out). Congrats to Heather Reed, Marilyn Mateo, James Black, Charles Clarke & Paul Kelly. Next week the 9-Tap Jackpot will be $12,194.00, just shy of our Tour record.

Kim Pohlot earned her 2nd Tour Championship earning her 'Red Jersey' on Sunday shooting an IMPRESSIVE 798. Making the needed adjustments from Saturday's tournament (where she was the low-to-cash at 699), she found her groove and didn't let of the gas. She says the support and cheering from Walt Dalrymple, Donnie Roberts, Earl Kleist, & Chuck Hufford is what kept her focused on keeping her Storm Surge (drilled by Robert Kelly) on the lane. What a Cinderella Story from being the cut-line one day, to topping the ladder the next day. She is going to use her $2,340.00 check to fund an upcoming vacation. Great Job Kim!!!

In 2nd Place, Dale Roberts Jr. made a FANTASTIC attempt for his Second Title. Shooting a nice 794, just FIVE pins away from taking the top rung, he found his line after the first game and just stuck with it. His 2nd Place check of $1,035.00, pushes his Career Earnings to $11,159.00.

Chris Hale had a tremendous run shooting a solid 793 series. Fairly new to the Tour, he was only five pins away from earning his 1st Championship and his third place position is the highest place he has earned to date. His $524.00 payday will definitely fund future events especially when he is that close to having his picture taken.

The 'Daily Double' was such a phenomenal event. We had a total of 85 participate both days, combining the highest series of Saturday and Sunday. Just a $5 investment to share in the extra Cash List paid pretty nice dividends. Paying out the Top 10 places for a sweet total of $3,154.00, Andy Snow earned a whooping $854.00 and the 'Daily Double' Champion Shirt and a New STORM Ball. Congratulations to all the bowlers that participated and maybe we will see this format again in the future.

Thank you to everyone that participated in the Storm Gear Raffle this weekend. We were able to raise over $1,500.00 to add toward the 'Daily Double' Pot. Congratulations to those who won the drawings: Andrey Williams, Cameron Figg, Will Andrews, Alyssa Wight, Andy Snow, James Mitchell, Kim Pohlot, Joe Fincher & Cheneil Lopez.
