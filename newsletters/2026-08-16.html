ONE-PIN WIN & Family Affair
$500.00 Paid 'Instant $20'

While we did spread around an extra $500.00 of Jackpot money at Lucky Strike Lynnwood, we had expected to hand out even more. It was lots of fun, so we'll look at doing the 'Instant $20' again soon, as long as the 9-Tap Jackpot remains so HUGE. As we predicted, scores were again on the light side at Lynnwood, with no one reaching 800 Saturday. The low scores historically have led to some really tight competitions, and this Tourney lived up to that trend. We had a number of ONE PIN margins including our Champion winning by just ONE PIN, 4th & 5th ONE-PIN. Even the Scratch Pot Series Winner was ONE-PIN & High Game was ONE-PIN. For another version of 'Closeness', we had a Cash List of 36 Members (tie for 35th) that was full of family members... finishing side-by-side were Father & Son Bill 9th & Kalym 8th Henry, also FOUR Husband & Wife cashers Red 24th & Marilyn 10th Mateo, David 30th & Young 24th Pettengill, Andre 21st & Crisanta 12th Williams, Anthony 14th & Irene 16th Balane, not to be outdone Brothers Thang 2nd & Huyen 19th Thai, a true 'Family Affair' this week!!

Despite the extra $500.00 paid out from the 'Instant $20', 9-Tap Jackpot stayed above $12K for Westside next week. Kim Pohlot came close to taking down the Jackpot, stringing 7-Taps in the Front 8 frames, one errant shot costing her a very large bundle. Yanis Green instead had his Front-5 Taps hold up worth a nice $375.00 cash.

Our Lynnwood Champion was David Suom, who won his first Tour Title just 6 weeks ago. He's been working to improve his game, which has certainly paid off, evidenced by his average improving by 13 pins just since May. The first Title came at Acme on the String-Pins, where the scores were much higher than they were at Lynnwood. David shared that he was disappointed by a 7 count in the 11th frame... not realizing that he needed to pick up all three pins for the Win. He shook off the frustration and refocused to thankfully convert the spare providing him with his ONE-PIN Win. When asked what he might do with his $2,131.00 earnings, his romantic response was, "Taking my wife out on a nice date." He also shared his thanks to his close friends Johnny Seng & Tina Tran. In addition to his job at UW, David likes to do work as a Community Volunteer.

In 2nd Place, Thang Duc Thai broke out of a bit of a slump to shoot his best series since April, while most other bowlers were shooting lower totals at Lynnwood. We're sure he can think of many frames where he could have picked up that one extra pin that he needed for 1st Place. A Win would have been the 16th of his amazing career, while his $957.00 check moves his prize money total to $73,134.00, putting him 4th on the Tour's All-Time list.

In 3rd Place, Alex Cady continued what has been a HOT year for him. Alex was gunning for his 4th career Title which would have earned him a Gold color Champion Jersey. I'm sure he has that Jersey in his sights for the near future. His 3rd Place money of $498.00 this week, pushes his young career total to $13,213.00, as he continues to climb toward the $20,000.00 CLUB.
