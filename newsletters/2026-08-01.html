On Sunday, everyone is eligible to bowl when we host our 3rd Leon Bemis Memorial to honor our Tour Hall-of-Fame Member and 18-year Floor Director. On Sunday, we'll respond to the request from many of our bowlers with a 'Long-Oil' pattern (42ft). As always, since this is not a normal lane condition, if the overall scoring is out of whack (high or low), we won't count the scores for average purposes, so go crazy & have fun. With the AWESOME items we have in the Raffle, it's already generated enough ADDED-MONEY that it looks like we'll be paying 10 to 12 places in the combined Cash List. The Raffle continues on Sunday and we'll do the drawings before the 3pm Squad tomorrow.

Saturday was a great competition with the Top 3 separated by just SEVEN pins. A nice 181 entries generated payouts of $12,994.00. MANY shot scores that will put them in a good position to make the 'Daily Double' Cash List, when they combine their Sunday scores. The 9-Tap Jackpot nearly got hit (6 of Front-7) & the Middle-7 also was a near miss by another new Member Seth Ziolkowski. Instead, FIVE bowlers tied with the Front-3 Taps, earning $75.00 each ($375.00 total paid out).

15-year Tour Member Andy Snow returns to the Winner's Circle for the first time in several years. This is Title #7 for Andy, with his sweet $2,322.00 in earnings Saturday, pushing his Career total to an impressive $35,464.00 in prize money. That doesn't even include the well over $10,000.00 he's earned from the 9-Tap Jackpot, which he's one of the few bowlers to have hit it more than once. At Secoma, he stayed very consistent for all three games, shooting just enough to slip by with a THREE-pin margin of Victory. Nice Job Andy!!

In 2nd Place, Johnny Seng made a GREAT run at his SIXTH Title. When he reached the 7th frame of his final game, he needed to STRIKEOUT the rest of the way for a TIE. Johnny got them all... except for the final shot. A 7 count left him 3 pins short, but it was a super effort to make it close. His nice 2nd Place check of $1,034.00, pushes his Career Earnings to $33,862.00, very similar to Andy's.

3rd Place was the other end of the spectrum from Andy & Johnny. Newcomer Beau Coy earned his first payday on the Tour. Pretty nice when your first check is a podium finish. A $541.00 payday is a great way to get off the ground... maybe in a few years, Beau can join Andy & Johnny in the $30,000.00 CLUB. Beau's nice 791 series left him only 7 pins short of his first Win.

This is by far the largest collection of Raffle items we've had, including a Kyle Troup autographed PBA game-used Storm ball, a Storm shammy signed by eight PBA & PWBA Tour members, a Seattle Seahawks ball, Doria Pajak's game-used ball and jersey, Stu Williams' jersey and two Parker Bohn autographed pins.
