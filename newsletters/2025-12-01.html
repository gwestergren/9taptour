9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundDecember 1, 2025
21st First-Time Champ Crowned
Jackpot Gets a Few Bruises

Well the Holiday Season is upon us and the end of 2025 is near. With just ONE opportunity left in 2025 to earn your automatic invitation to the Year-End Championship. Entries remained at impressive numbers at Secoma Lanes, with a wonderful 242 entries and another huge payout total at $15,366.00. Scores ranged from moderate to slightly low, with only 3 bowlers topping 800 and a nice low 'Cut Line' of just 676. The THREE 800's were all tossed by bowlers looking for their 1st Tour Title and the automatic invite to the YEC that came with the Win.

Well, the 9-Tap Jackpot proved to be a challenge for everyone this week. The Pot only gave up a few nibbles, when FIVE bowlers hit the Front-3 Taps, earning $75.00 each, Dale Roberts Jr, Clemencia Lopez, Robert Galdo, Isaac Joseph & Montana Xaysana. There's one more chance in 2025 to show the Jackpot who's the boss. Next week it climbs back over $10K at $10,333.00.

Well, for the 21st time this year the Tour crowned a first-time champion! Congratulations to Jeremy Carnahan. Jeremy certainly struggled through his first seven entries on the Tour... but like a high voltage light switch, he sure lit it up on try #8, shooting 849 for the win! After being out of bowling for many years, it obviously didn't take him long to find that elusive 'Groove' that we all look for regularly. That 'Groove' was electrifying with all three games topping his previous best score. He said he was lucky and nervous, because he had never bowled that well before. Jeremy wants to thank Randy and staff at Bowlero (Lakewood) Pro Shop where his ball was drilled. He earned a hefty $2,568.00 and plans to buy more bowling gear for himself for Christmas. Congratulations Jeremy!

Taking second place earning $1,167.00 was Eric Alexander. Like Jeremy, he was pushing to get his 1st Tour Title. While holding the lead after the 2nd squad, he was especially excited about getting that YEC Invite if his score held up. He was close, shooting a great 821 just behind Jeremy. Eric joined the Tour Family in February 2025 and by a large margin this was his best finish.

Rounding out the Top 3 was Jaqua Arrington shooting an excellent 806, earning him a hefty $580.00 to put in his pocket to help with his Holiday shopping. Jaqua joined the Tour in late 2023 and this was also his best finish.
