KENMORE LANES has been a HOT HOUSE for the Tour lately. We've had THREE straight BIG payouts from the 9-Tap Jackpot, along with THREE straight BIG turnouts & THREE straight Tourney's with incredibly CONSISTENT scoring. With an impressive average of 209 Entries per Tournament, we've set new standards at Kenmore repeatedly and as a result, the payouts have been pretty nice as well. The $14,486.00 we've averaged in payouts was certainly helped out by the Jackpot getting hit for $750.00 in all three events. First it was James Black hitting 7-Middle Taps for $750.00, then it was Mary Bridgman getting the Front-6 and a 'Golden Shot' collecting $750.00 in Cash, then in July it was Miles Nerona tossing the Middle-7 for the $750.00 Bonus. We've also had $750.00 payouts in our last 2 Tourneys, so it seems like our bowlers are determined to take down the Jackpot before the year ends. For good measure, the scoring has been nearly perfect at Kenmore, with nearly identical winning scores of 834, 834 & 832, while the 'Cut-Lines' have been within the 'Goldilocks' range at 693, 705 & 720. We're hoping for more of the same on Sunday.

A GREAT time of the year to refer a fellow bowler to join the Tour.

CONGRATULATIONS to Ruong Sa Tang for becoming the 50th Member of the $20,000.00 CLUB!! Being the 50th makes it even more of a special milestone for both Ruong and the Tour. In addition to Ruong's two Tour Titles, won at Skyway and Bowlero-Acme, he has been a very steady Casher over the years. His current Prize Money earnings stand at $20,140.00, which doesn't even reflect that he's also done well with earnings from the Bracket Table and in Jackpot Bonuses (2 Big Jackpot paydays this year) as well.
