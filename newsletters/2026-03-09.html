9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundMarch 9, 2026
The 'Year of the Rookies'
Record Volume on Brackets

Wow, super showing for the 1st squad Sunday at Secoma. There were 107 entries vying for the ball giveaway. Congratulations to Mary Bridgman and Nicholas Pagdilao with the High Handicap Games earning them the balls. With the large turnout of 262 Entries, it also created a huge increase in the 9-Tap Jackpot, pushing it to a nice $3,518.00 for next week's 1QC.

Scoring was right on track, with only three 800's and a 691 cut-line. We were able to pay out a whopping 53 spots with a total payout of $17,780.00. As seems to be the trend for the year, sharing was abundant with a RECORD percentage of ties, 33 of the 53 cashers were involved in ties.

The bracket Gals were sure busy with a RECORD volume of $5,357.00 and a near record for the Scratch Pot of $950.00. Thank you all for your patience and support of our new bracket ladies, Nichole, Chloe & Lynn.

Well, for the 8th time in 10 events and the 5th consecutive Tourney, we crown another 1st-Time Champion! To add to the excitement the three top finishers were all going for their first championship.

This week's Rookie Champion is Eric Stevens. Since joining the Tour family in September 2025, Eric has managed to cash a couple of times. Sporting his Dexter SST8 shoes along with his Hammer Hazmat Pearl, he was able to put together an impressive 849 for the win earning himself a sweet $2,497.00. He plans to buy a new ball for dry conditions or aggressive back-ends with some of his winnings. Thanks go out to his daughter and the Cady family for cheering him on.

Another newcomer who has been working his way toward the top of the Cash List, is Collin Dionne. Collin joined the Tour back in November 2025 and is definitely another 'rookie' to watch. Shooting an excellent 813 series Sunday earned him his biggest payday yet, with a great check for $1,114.00.

In 3rd place, Joshua Brown was actually the Veteran Tour Member of the Top 3, having joined almost exactly one year ago. Joshua put together the only other 800 series of the day. On a re-entry, his personal best series to date of 806 earned him a healthy check for $593.00.
