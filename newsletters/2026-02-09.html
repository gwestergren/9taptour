SUPERBOWL DAY at DAFFODIL

Thank you to all that came out to join us on Superbowl Sunday! Bowlers came out in superbowl fashion and filled the first two squads to the max. As predicted just as 9-Tap bowlers are dedicated to the Tour they are just as dedicated to the Seahawks as our final squad was definitely on the low side.

The 9-Tap Jackpot got off fairly easy again this week, with Manuel Mason getting the Front-5 Taps in the 3rd game of the final squad, giving him $250.00 take home. So, the Pot grows for next week, which also keeps the 7-Middle Taps in play as well.

Another 1st time Champion is crowned. Since joining the Tour in November 2025 today's champion, Yanis Green, has been determined to take home a title. After finishing tied for 2nd place at Kent, Yanis was more determined this week to claim a title. He came out guns a blazing using a Storm Pitch Blue that was given to him by his dad. Shooting 834 earned Yanis $2,241.00 and will help him purchase the Storm IQ78U that he wants and his entry fee for the upcoming PBA Regional. He is most grateful to his dad and grandfather, Osmond Green (9-Tap Hall of Fame Member) for teaching him to bowl.

In 2nd Place is Calvin Jones, joining the Tour just two weeks after Yanis, fell just a bit short shooting 827 earning him a check for $1,006.00. Since joining the Tour, Calvin has appeared on the cash list numerous times. Definitely someone you want to watch for.

Rounding out the top three is veteran, Jeremy Lanouette shooting 816 and taking home $525.00.
