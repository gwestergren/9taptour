9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundOctober 5, 2026
Record 21st First-Time Champ!!!

In what has become the 'Year of the Rookies' We tied last year's modern record, with our 21st 1st-Time Champion, with a whole quarter still to go in 2026. What a great way to return from our "Bye Week," with the Seahawks dismantling the Chargers and the beautiful sunny weather, we couldn't have asked for a more PERFECT day of bowling. We had a phenomenal turn out of 211 entries for our Secoma Tournament, which paid out over $14K. Scores were outstanding with a perfect Cut-Line of 693 and three bowlers topping 800. It is safe to say that our bowlers were on a mission to take the top spot.

The 9-Tap Jackpot was showing it's tough exterior today with only the Front-4 being the top consolation for most of the day. Jeremy Lanouette and Malou Aninion had a chance for the Middle-7 but a real strike hindered their progress. However, on the LAST throw, of the LAST game, of the LAST bowler, of the day...Willie Manning Jr. was able to string the Middle-7 earning him the top consolation of the day and a nice $750.00 payday. The 9-Tap Jackpot grew slightly to $11,095.00 for next weekend. Great Job Everyone.

In what has become the 'Year of the Rookie Champs', Charles Daughery Jr tied last year's modern record by becoming our 21st 1st-Time Champion of 2026, and we have a whole quarter still to go to shatter that record. Previously, Charles had EIGHT Top-5 finishes including a 2nd & THREE 3rd place finishes. Shooting a fantastic 849 series FINALLY launched him to the top spot that he's been fighting for since joining the 9-Tap Tour. He said he was "in the Zone," and everything was just working for him. He wants to thank his wife Amy Silverhorn and lane mates Stephanie Lanouette, Paul Butorajac, and Jeffrey Price for their continued support and motivation throughout the Tournament. He takes home a nice $2,627.00 and also earns his Blue Champion Jersey, which he intends to wear at every future event. Congratulations!!!

Mike Miller needed a little time to warm up and figure out the lanes. He made some mental notes, re-entered, and applied the right adjustments to shoot a great 830 series, securing his 2nd Place position. His $1,160.00 check moves his lifetime cash to over $15K. Great shooting Mike!!

Earl Kleist is no stranger to competition. Shooting a nice 801 series, he set the bar for the other bowlers to emulate. His 3rd place position earned him a nice $585.00 for his performance and pushes his amazing lifetime cash to $90,483.00, putting Earl on the doorstep of becoming the Tour's SECOND career $100,000.00 earner.
