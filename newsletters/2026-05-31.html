Women Taking Over Tour!!!

What an exciting weekend with our Tour traveling to Lynnwood. Despite the traffic, construction, weather, and Mariners game... we had an outstanding 175 entries. Throughout the squads we saw lots of high games rolled, but only two bowlers put together a BIG series by shooting over 800. The 'Cut-Line' was the same after all 3 squads holding at 726 throughout the day. A little higher than usual, due to a minor glitch with the oil machine.

The 9-Tap Jackpot is steadily increasing each week. With only two bowlers, Kahlee Barrio and Dale Roberts Jr able to string the Front 4-Taps to take home $150.00 each. Anthony Balane was close to shooting Middle-7 Taps twice during his entries, while Ed Berg had a string of 6-Taps in the middle, giving him the chance to throw ONE BALL for the $750.00 Bonus. The Jackpot increased to $9,031.00 for next week.

Well, the women continue to top the ladder for the 2nd week in-a-row, with Young Pettengill claiming her first individual Title. Last year, she and husband David won the Dad's Day Doubles. Young shot an amazing 823 series, but needed a strong finish in her final game 9th & 10th frames. A 'turkey' did the trick giving her a 16 pin winning margin. She said she felt like she was in the zone during that squad but had to make some quick last game adjustments to secure the top spot. She will be taking home $2,867.00 which she will use to take her grandchildren shopping.

Shooting 807 and claiming 2nd Place was Dale Roberts Jr. He has been on the best run of his Tour career over the past few months, including his first Title earlier this year. He was definitely locked in and was in position to make a run at the lead. He earned a hefty $1,272.00, to boost his career earnings very close to the $10K mark.

Joel Brown shot a great 799 securing 3rd Place and taking home a nice $648.00. It was his best finish of the year as well, for the one-time Champion.

Look forward to seeing everyone at Bowlero Lanes in Tacoma.
