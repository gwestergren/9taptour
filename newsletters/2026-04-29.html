PBA at AOW 10 Days Ago
'GOLDEN SHOTS' Twice Last Three
Saturday - 11:00 & 1:30 & 4:00

Angel of the Winds has become so popular with our bowlers, it's earned a spot on our regular Tour schedule for several years now. Almost exactly one year ago, we were at AOW (May 11th), setting an amazing House record with 194 Entries. Some of our bowlers think it's a long trip, but according to Google Maps, it's only 1hr. 15 min. drive from Tacoma (no traffic). If you've been there before, you're aware of what a beautiful facility it is. This will be our first visit in 2026 and we've been hearing from many of our Members that have been looking forward to Angel of the Winds this weekend. The PBA Tour was at AOW again just 10 days ago, finishing their Tourney on the 19th.

GOLDEN SHOTS: TWO of our last THREE visits to AOW have produced 'Golden Shots' at the 9-Tap Jackpot. Walt Dalrymple & Earl Kleist each earned a shot at Tap #7 on the AOW lanes. In Earl's case, the Jackpot was sitting at $12,106.00, very near the All-Time Record. That made Earl's 'Golden Shot', at the time, the biggest in Tour History. Here's how we described it then, "An EERIE silence grew as Earl prepared for his Big Throw... he threw what looked to most of us like a perfect ball, but it only carried an 8-count." Walt's shot was very similar, in that we thought it looked like a winner, but unfortunately the ball finished too strong resulting in a split. Maybe the 3rd-Time will be the charm at AOW... but who might be throwing the shot?

We made a small adjustment to the Oil Pattern last time, in an effort to have the pattern hold up better throughout the day. The overall scoring didn't seem to be affected, with our last 3 'Cut-Lines' at 706, 698 & 706, while our last Tourney only saw two 800's thrown. Hope to see more of the same Saturday!!
