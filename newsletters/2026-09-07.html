9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundSeptember 7, 2026
3rd Quarter 2026 Complete
First Time Champion Crowned!!!

Such a great weekend at Kent Bowl hosting our 3rd Quarter Championship for 2026. Following the competitiveness of our 2nd Quarter Championship event, we had an awesome 285 entries across two days of competition. We had a total payout of $20,475.00. Compared to Saturday's squads, Sunday's combined squad cut-off line was at 679 with both Joel Brown and Jim Barrio sneaking into the Finals due to previous qualifiers from Saturday.

The 9-Tap Jackpot took a little hit this weekend with two Front-5s hit by Greg Burdsall and Huyen Thai each earning $375.00. The Jackpot still grew, thanks to the huge turnout, continuing to increase the all-time record to $13,120.00. Maybe string-pins at Bowlero-Acme will crack the Jackpot.

Adam Dreller was able to lock in the weekend's Top Qualifier spot shooting an impressive 787. It took him all FIVE squads to find his line but it was all worth it for the new TQ Jacket.

Cal Fisher earned the Top Saturday Qualifier with a nice 768 series securing his new STORM ball to be thrown at future 9-Tap events, I wonder what ball he will get?

Fifty-eight Members advanced to the finals where we saw some awesome tight matches and some big scores. Top 14 Qualifiers earned themselves a 'BYE' during the first Round. From there the cuts began, with the bottom 14 going out after round 1, and continuing towards round 2, cutting 13, before working our way to the Sweet 16 finalists. When all was finally said and done, we crowned our 3rd Quarter Champion.

Our 3rd Quarter Champion, James Mitchell, was on fire throughout the finals. Joining the Tour in March 2025, he qualified with a 715 series and worked his way up from the first Monster Bracket, making great shots on his way to the Final head to head. He never let off the gas and continued pressuring his opponents. Throughout the stages he was advancing, he was getting super excited and had to remind himself to stay calm. His mental game was sharp and definitely paid off, earning his 1st CHAMPIONSHIP with his previous highest placement being 5th. James wants to thank Tyler at Rain City Bowling Supply for drilling his gear and having him ready for the tournament. He takes home a whopping $2,707.00 for all his hard work which he said he will use to "probably pay bills." Great Job and Congratulations on your HUGE WIN!!!

In 2nd Place, Joel Brown was consistent throughout the Tournament. He didn't hear that he had made the initial cut during the 1st Monster Bracket and had put all his gear in his car. At the start of the 2nd Monster Bracket he had to rush and grab his gear and didn't have any time to practice. He used that momentum to scale the ladder into the Finals. With a couple unlucky splints during the final game, he still shot an impressive 215. Just one game shy of earning his second championship he is definitely motivated for the next tournament. He will be bringing home a nice $1,213.00 to fund future Tournaments.

Our two 3rd Place bowlers James Black & Miles Nerona had impressive runs on their road to the Final 4. Both were winning head-to-head matches by sizeable margins, with some really nice games, up until the Final 4. They each brought home $451.00. Great Job from you both!
