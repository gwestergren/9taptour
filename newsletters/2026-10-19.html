Back to Back Wins
and Middle-7 Taps!!!

Had a nice turn-out at West Seattle Bowl, especially with the sun and Mt. Rainer peaking out. We had 155 entires, which paid out $11,924. Scores were phenomenal with a PERFECT Cut-Line of 699 and at least one 800 shot in every squad. Definitely had some great games and a significant amount of 9-Tap strings that kept everyone on edge.

For the THIRD weekend in-a-row, the 9-Tap Jackpot was hit for $750.00. The third try was the charm at shooting for the Middle-7 on Sunday. Cory Pagdilao & Thang Duc Thai each earned the chance to throw one ball for $750.00 but missed getting a 9-Tap on the Money Ball. Cory commented about how nervous she was on her last throw, while Thang tossed a real strike on #7, he got his 7th Tap one frame too late.

Teddy Drake was the 3rd bowler with a big Money Shot. She pounced on the opportunity and was able to convert on the 7th shot earning her the top consolation for a nice $750.00. CONGRATULATIONS!!!

If you are not tracking, we have not only paid out THREE Middle-7 Taps in a row, we've also had SEVEN Middle-7s hit in the last 6 events, paying out a whopping $6,450.00 in those 6 Tourneys. The 9-Tap Jackpot took a slight hit this weekend but remains a solid $11,064.00 for next weekend. Great Job Everyone.

As we have seen over the years, many bowlers have gotten into the 'Zone' where they get really HOT for a few weeks. Many times we've had back-to-back Winners in the Tour's history and once we even had a three-peat Champ. Amazingly, in Samson's 25 years on the Tour, he has often found that infamous 'Zone' where he's been on a nice streak. But, the Tour's All-Time leading Money Earner and Title Leader has only ONCE previously won Back-to-Back Titles and that came way back in 2009, which fittingly also started with a win at Bowlero Lanes. This time his Bowlero Win was followed by West Seattle Bowl, where he last Won in 2013. I guess purple is his lucky color as he is wearing his purple Tour shirt for his 38th Title. Bowling with a -5 pins of handicap, he shot an impressive 833 in the 1st squad, holding off a few assaults to take home $2,577.00 for his effort. Great Job!!!

Jeremy Adolphsen found a line that just worked for him at WSB. ONLY FIVE pins separated him from the top spot. His missed spare in the first frame of the last game kept him from surpassing Samson. He still shot an amazing 828 series and even cashed on the Scratch Pots. This is his highest placing since joining the Tour last year. He took home a nice $1,136.00 for 2nd Place. Awesome Performance!

Teddy Drake made an impressive effort, stringing great games toward her 3rd Place finish. Just a few marks away from her first Championship, it won't be long before we see her earn her Blue Championship Shirt. She still took home the $750.00 Jackpot Consolation to go with her 3rd Place check for $567.00. Not a bad pay day.
