9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundJuly 26, 2026
2nd Star Earned...
A New Contender Emerges!!!

Another great weekend for the 9-Tap Tour, as we headed to Bowlero Lanes. Despite other tournaments happening this weekend, like the PBA Regional at Secoma, we had an impressive turn out of 162 entires that paid out over $11K with a perfect Cut-Line of 702. We only had two 800s this weekend and a vast range of 700s that shows how well both the lanes and our bowlers performed.

The 9-Tap Jackpot finally showed a kink in its armor, after withstanding many major assault for months. Ed Tuttle lead early with just the Front-3, while both Earl Kleist and Malou Aninion were close to hitting the Middle-7 Taps but were thwarted by a strike on their seventh shot.

Our newest member Tyler Jackson was able to string not just 7, but 8-Middle Taps to earn the $750.00 consolation prize. Tyler wasn't even sure what he had just accomplished and was more focused on closing out his 10th frame. Great Job Tyler. The Jackpot will be $11,574.00 for our next event at Secoma Lanes.

David Pettengill came to Bowlero with a mission... he was going to take 1st place, as his wife Young Pettengill had predicted. On their way to Bowlero she said she had a feeling he would do great this weekend. Taking those words to heart, along with something he learned from last weekend's event with the string pins, he made those adjustments and RAN AWAY with his 2nd Title. David said he had an 'aha' moment last week at Bowlero-Acme and used his higher performance ball, but just made adjustments faster to stay in the pocket. Well, it sure paid off, since he tossed both his highest series (by a bunch) and highest game that he's shot since joining the Tour 6 1/2 years ago. He wants to thank his wife Young for all her support. They both earned their first Title during the 'Dads Doubles' event in 2025. His 870 earned him a great payday worth $2,259.00. When asked what he was going to do with his check... he immediately handed the check to his wife!!!

Tyler Jackson is a New Member on the Tour, referred by his Dad, Ron Jackson. Being his first 9-Tap Event, he didn't know what to expect, but what an amazing showing. He shot his highest game 290 which included his Middle-7 (as mentioned above), also shooting the only other 800 series of the day, with an impressive 818. Tyler was close to doing something that hasn't been done since 2016... we haven't had a New Member win a Title in over 10 years. Tyler has undoubtedly thrown his hat into the arena and everyone should be on the look out for this new contender. He took home a nice $767.00 check which I can see funding future events on the Tour.

Joe Fincher Jr. shot an outstanding 798 for his 3rd Place position. Joe often has strong performances during our events, as he did again with his scores yesterday. His $524.00 check bumps his career earnings to $26,606.00.

Next week we will have a Special Two-Tourney weekend at Secoma Lanes. Watch for Wednesday's Newsletter for more info.
