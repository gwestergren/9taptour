WOW, another great week for the Tour. No Progressive Pot in the Tour's 'Modern Era' (after 2005) has been so BIG and the Top 3 finishers capitalized on the opportunity! Even past '2-Day money Added' events and Tourneys with over 300 entries never came close to Sunday's Top 3 Payouts, each setting 20 year highs.

Well, after taking a beating last week the 9-Tap Jackpot is taking charge again holding NINE bowlers at bay only allowing the Front-3 Taps this week. Tap #4 proved to be difficult for everyone. Each of the nine bowlers took home $25 for their efforts. How long will it take this time for someone to show the Pot who's the Boss? Only time will tell...

Well, week two of the New Year and we already have our second First-Time Champion! Congratulations to Ty Richardson!! Ty took full advantage of the high Progressive Pot, shooting a GREAT 829 series, one of only TWO 800's shot Sunday. He took home a whopping $3,782.00, the biggest Champion Check since 2005. Before today, Ty's highest finish since joining the Tour in July 2024, was 4th place. But when it comes to Quarterly and Year End Championship Events in 2025, it was a different story. He finished 5th at the 2nd Qtr Championship, 9th at the 3rd Qtr Championship and 5th at the Year-End Championship. Not only did he finish 5th at the YEC he was also the Top Qualifier winning a new Storm Ball. That ball proved to be a gem as it helped him with today's win. Ty's winnings will allow him and his new wife Adriana to take a beautiful honeymoon. Congratulations Ty!

Taking 2nd Place with a sweet 814 is Manuel Mason. Also taking advantage of the high Progressive Pot, Manuel earned $1,623.00 for his efforts. Like Ty, Manuel also has only been with the Tour for just over a year and he was looking for Win #1 as well. Looks like these guys are finding their groove and are definitely ones to keep our eyes on.

By comparison, the final person to take advantage of the large pot in 3rd Place is an oldtimer. Brett Harrison has been a part of the Tour family for a whole year longer than Ty and Manuel. All three were chasing their 1st Title. Shooting a solid 791 put Brett in position to also take home an enormous $850.00 3rd Place share.
