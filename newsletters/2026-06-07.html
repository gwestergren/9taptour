9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundJune 7, 2026
TWO $750.00 Shots
New Lane Machine at
Bowlero Lanes (Lakewood)

Bowlero got a new oil machine, so even using the exact same pattern, it played just a little different than the old machine did. Although the 'Cut-Line' was a little higher this week, we also had five 800's tossed, with at least one coming from every squad. The 9-Tap Jackpot had a couple scares, with Charles Clarke and Dan Vance each with an opportunity to throw ONE BALL for the Middle-7 Taps and $750.00 in cash. Charles unfortunately couldn't quite get the 9th pin to fall on his 'money shot' and Dan was a little too accurate, carrying all 10 pins. Dan teased himself further by getting another Tap on his 8th shot. Ashley Kochel was able to claim a little of the pot, hitting the Front-4 for $150.00. The pot makes another BIG jump to a nice $9,648.00 for Kenmore.

Serina Reese is an avid sports fan so no surprise how dedicated she is to bowling. To Serina "Bowling is Life". When she's not on the lanes she's supporting the Seattle Sounders, Seawolves or Kraken any way possible. Saturday morning, Serina said she woke up feeling pretty good, which clearly paid off for her. She found her line early getting into that famous 'Zone'. Her 827, shot in the first squad, landed her in the top spot right away. Serina hung out all afternoon, as 4 more 800s were thrown while she anxiously watched. During the final squad Samson Ibale found his line, making her VERY nervous as he finished within 6 pins. With her boyfriend, Michael Pruitt by her side, she was able to hang on and claim her first Tour Title earning her a check for a sweet $2,343.00. No surprise that the sports fan plans on using some for a trip to a FIFA World Cup Game.

Falling just a bit short, finishing in 2nd Place with a great 821 is Tour Hall of Fame bowler Samson Ibale. Earning a nice check for $1,052.00, pushes his career earnings to over $127,000.00. With a mind blowing 36 Titles on his resume and his dedication to the sport, he came close to adding another Title to that resume.

Rounding out the Top 3, shooting a strong 813 earning him $569.00 is Jay Pannette. You've seen Jay's name on the Cash List a lot lately during his recent Hot Streak. A 5 1/2 year Member, Jay has ONE previous Title and wasn't far off Title number two Saturday.
