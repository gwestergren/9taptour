9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundSeptember 14, 2026
20th First Time Champ Crowned!!!

What a wonderful weekend we had with the start of the NFL season and the beautiful PNW rain coming down on Sunday. We had a nice turn out of 126 entries for our Bowlero-Acme Tournament, which included our "Front-2 Insta $20." We were able to payout a little over $11K with a little lower Cut-Line of 677. No 800s were thrown this go around but scores were definitely competitive throughout th day.

The 9-Tap Jackpot took a significant hit this weekend as the string-pins consistently show the perfect relationship it has with 9 counts. Thomas Donahue started the attack with hitting the Front-6 and missing the 7th with an 8 count. Earl Kleist was able to string the Middle-8 and would have gone more until he ran out of room. Michelle Galdo knocked down the Front-6 and hit a real strike on the 7th frame. Each of them took away $750.00. The 'Insta $20' was hit for $600.00 total with 24 members taking advantage of the Front-2 with Red Mateo, Thang Duc Thai, Earl Kleist, Tarry Rohr, and Cesar Lim cashing multiple times. We paid out a total of $2,850 from our 9-Tap Jackpot knocking it down to $10,790 for next weekend. Great Job Everyone.

Crowning our 20th First time Champion this year, Yoel Green came to our event with one mentality, "I'm going to beat my brother Yanis." Struggled during his first entry he hesitated to re- enter. He talked with his brother and eventually re-entered and applied what he learned from his previous performance. He found a line and didn't let off the pressure. He set both a new personal best for a 282 HDCP Game and a 775 Series which led him to the top of the leaderboard. He wants to thank his brother Yanis for encouraging him to bowl and joining the Tour. He will be taking home a nice $1,823.00 which he intends to use to purchase new equipment in preparation for the Year-End Championship. Congratulations!!!

Thang Duc Thai was on fire throughout the day and even took advantage of the 'Insta $20.' Shooting a great 767, he was just one mark shy of taking over the top spot. His $1,158.00 check gets him a few dollars short of his lifetime cash to $75K. Great shooting!!

Tarry Rohr also took advantage of the 'Insta $20' and continued to pressure the pins. He shot a nice 752 series sneaking into third place by 2 pins. He will be taking home $588.00 for his performance.

Next week we will be at Paradise Lanes shooting our Tour favorite 'Best 3 out of 4' format. Looking forward to seeing everyone there.
