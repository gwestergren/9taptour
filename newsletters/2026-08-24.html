9-Tap Tour - Puget SoundAugust 24, 2026
17th First Time Champ crowned
at Westside Lanes after 18 years!!!

What a FANTASTIC event we had this weekend at Westside Lanes. After the 18-year hiatus, we had a few of our tour bowlers reminisce of the last time they had bowled in Olympia and looked forward to how the lanes rolled. We greatly appreciate the Kirstie and the staff at Westside Lanes in supporting us for this event and ensuring the lanes were ready for the competition. With 166 entries and a cut-line at 697, our bowlers scored the PERFECT spread of scores throughout the day to include three 800s that topped the leader's board, each of them vying for their first championship.

We had SO MANY strings of Taps being shot all day, that there seemed to be every combination possible except for a BIG PAYDAY. We took so many pictures of those strings that we lost track of who shot them all. Several bowlers had 6 of 7 Taps, a few had NINE in one game. Earl even had SIX Taps in-a-row, but ran out of frames at the end of the game. The one big money shot was Osvaldo Gonzalez, going for Middle-7 Taps, earning the chance to throw ONE BALL for $750.00...he threw a great shot, but an 8-count ruined his payday. Front 4-Taps took the consolation with Brett Harrison and David Pettengill taking home $150.00 each. The 9-Tap Jackpot has now reached $12,636.00 breaking the record by $10.00.

William Poole woke up Saturday morning with one thought "I am going to place today," well that manifested with a phenomenal 825 series and becoming the 17th First Time Champion this year. William said he was in the zone and the lanes were working in his favor. Joining the tour 2 years ago, his last highest place was last year when he tied for 2nd and was working on his game to reach the top. He wants to thank the "Winner's Circle" for their support including Willie Manning Jr., Patricia Beed, and Bria Johnson. He is taking home a fantastic $2,142.00 check and already looking to use some the earnings to adding another championship under his belt when he goes to Hi Joy Bowl next week. Congratulations!!!!

In 2nd Place, Joyce Roberts took a break from the registration table, found a line on the lanes and just continued to punish it. She was on path to taking the top spot but needed a double in the 10th frame of the last game. She was able to get the first strike but ended with a 8-count split on the second ball that left her 5 pins shy of her first title with an 820. She stated she can wait till Hi Joy Bowl to get her first championship. Her 2nd place check of $743.00 check moves her prize money closer to the $10K milestone. Great Job!

In 3rd Place, Seth Ziolkowski continues to make an impact on the Tour. Having only joined the tour 3 weeks ago, this week he bowled his first Tour 300 and set both game and series PRs at 804. His 3rd Place money of $493.00 is just the start and the rest of the tour should be looking out for Seth on the top of the leader board in the future.

Don't forget we will be at Hi Joy Bowl next week playing on their newly installed string-pins. Look forward to seeing everyone next week and thanks to everyone that showed up to Westside Lanes.
