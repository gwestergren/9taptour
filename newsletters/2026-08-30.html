Newest Tour Member Becomes
18th First Time Champ!!!

Such a great weekend coming back to Hi Joy Bowl to test out their new string-pins and challenge our bowlers. We had 180 entries for the day with the cut-line at just 667, paying out a nice $11,866.00 for the Tournament. Scoring was a little on the low side Saturday, similar to our first Tournament on the string-pins at Bowlero-Acme. A small adjustment to Acme's oil pattern brought the scores right back to the 'Goldilocks' zone that we like to see. We'll make the same adjustment next time we visit Hi Joy Bowl. We did see some high games shot throughout the day with James Black shooting a 300 with 10 straight real strikes. He came close to matching Tour Member Josh Brown, who just a few weeks ago threw Hi Joy Bowl's first "Perfect 300" game on the new String-Pins during league play. The Tour has produced SEVEN sanctioned 300's over the years, but none in the last 5 years.

The 9-Tap Jackpot continues to elude our bowlers. Brother and Sister, Jeremy & Stephanie Lanouette, came close to making a dent in the Jackpot but each had 6 of the needed Middle-7 Taps. Congratulations to Jeremy Lanouette, Thang Duc Thai, Luis Micheri, and Samson Ibale for stringing the Front-4 Taps each taking home $150.00 for the highest Consolation. The 9-Tap Jackpot continues to grow, now at $12,719.00 on our way to the 3rd Quarter Championship at Kent Bowl next week.

Rare feat and a strange coincidence... 15 years ago, Carl Nichols joined the Tour and won his FIRST title on the very same day he joined. Carl visited us for the first time in a few years, during the 1st squad on Saturday. We talked about him being one of the very few Tour members to ever win a Championship on their first day. The only time since was in 2016 by Jenna Thompson. Shortly after that conversation, Chanyn Pauole, tossed the only 800 of the day to become only the THIRD brand new Member to Win since 2011.

Chanyn Pauole showed up to Hi Joy Bowl through an invitation from Alex Cady to try out 9-Tap after they bowled summer league. After bowling the first squad, he adjusted and re-entered. Using his Storm Purple Concept, he found his line and shot an outstanding 814 earning the top spot on the leader board and winning his first Championship Shirt on his first day on Tour. He is thankful to both Troy Breeding and Alex Cady for introducing him to the Tour and is looking forward to using his 1st Place earnings of $1,644.00 (as a new Member only allowed $10 in Pro. Pot) to fund future 9-Tap entries. Congratulations!!!

In 2nd Place, Jerry Smith had a great look on the lanes and took his experience from Acme's string-pins to Port Orchard. Just a couple marks away from reaching his second Title, he was able to string enough great scores to lock in a nice 796 series. His $1,016.00 check increases his lifetime earnings to only $42.00 short of the $15K milestone. Great Job.

In 3rd Place, Alex Cady continues to climb both the cash list and Bowler of the Year points. Shooting an impressive 789 series to secure 3rd place, which will certainly help his effort to finish in the B.O.Y Top 10. He takes home a nice $524.00 that he will be using in future entries.

Next weekend we will be at Kent Bowl for our 3rd Quarter Championship. Thank you for everyone that showed up Saturday and looking foward to seeing everyone at our next event.
