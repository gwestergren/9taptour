Women Reigning Supreme!!!

Wow what a weekend! Our 2nd Quarter Championship event did not disappoint as we had an awesome 295 entries, just shy of our all time record. The lanes for both days were both fun and challenging with each squad having very different cut-lines. David Sims was able to lock both the Top Saturday Qualifier for a new STORM ball as well as the weekend's Top Qualifier spot earning him the new TQ Jacket.

Fifty-Nine Members advanced to the finals where we saw some awesome tight matches and some big scores. Top 15 Qualifiers earned themselves a 'BYE' during the first Round. From there the cuts began, with the bottom 14 going out after round 1, and continuing towards round 2, cutting 13, before working our way to the Sweet 16 finalists. Strangely, we had a record total of four straight 9th/10th roll-offs after last spot ties in each of the first 4 Finals Rounds. When all was finally said and done, we crowned our 2nd Quarter Champion.

Our 2nd Quarter Champion, Stephanie Perrine worked hard to make it to the finals, starting by taking the last Qualifying spot in squad #1, shooting a 701 to secure her spot in the Finals. It was now she needed to lock in. Grinding through the various rounds she focused on hitting her target. Later, she mentioned that she was so focused that she didn't remember several matches and she'd never thrown that many 200 games before. It was during the round of 32 that she squeezed in by just 6 pins to move onto Sweet 16. She reminded herself to take it one game at a time until moving to the Championship match against Earl Kleist. Throwing her final ball she realized she hit a milestone and couldn't stop shaking. Stephanie earned a great $3,029.00 for all her hard work. She plans to use some of her winnings to pay off student loans and entering the Vegas Military Tournament in August. She told us that Saturday night she had a dream, that she was going to WIN...

The 9-Tap Jackpot took a little hit this weekend with two Front 5s hit by Jay Panette and Willy Downs each earning $250.00. But the Jackpot still grew thanks to the huge turnout. It has now surpassed $8K meaning that all Consolation Bonuses will be increased by 50% for next week.

In 2nd Place, Earl Kleist was consistent throughout the Tournament. An unlucky split derailed him in the final match from earning another star in his 24 years on the Tour. He was able to string the Middle 8-Taps in his Elite 8 match, which unfortunately didn't count during the Finals... but those strikes coupled with a dramatic 10th frame pick-up of a difficult 'washout', provided a narrow 6 pin victory. He will be bringing home $1,340.00 to fund future Tournaments.

Our two 3rd Place bowlers Tarry Rohr & William Hunter each had great runs all the way to the Final 4. Both were winning head-to-head matches by sizeable margins, with some really nice games, up until the Final 4.
