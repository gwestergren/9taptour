Great Win for 2002 Original
7-Middle Taps Returns

We had a brisk morning at Kent Bowl, while late in the afternoon we shared the day with an awesome Seahawks game. The Seahawks BIG Win was somewhat expected, Saturday's Tour Champion was a great surprise after a long dry spell. We had 165 entries, who produced an ideal range of scores, almost identical to our last visit.

Unlike last week's horde of bowlers hitting the Front-3 Taps, Ken Osborne Sr. stood alone stringing the Front 5-Taps. His string was halted when he flushed a strike in the 6th frame ending his run, but earning him $250.00 in cash. The 9-Tap Jackpot is now back over $4,000.00 so next week the 7-Middle Taps will be back in effect just in time for our debut at All-Star Lanes.

Ed Arneson is one of the Tour's original 2002 Members with Member #24. Over the years he's averaged entering around a dozen Tournaments per year, although last year he entered more than average. Last week at Bowlero he was on path to earning the Champion shirt but fell short on the third game. Ed, Rob and a number of other 9-Tap Tour members bowl in the South Sound Senior Travel League and several of them also bowl in the Friday "Pot Games" at Bowlero. Ed shot so well on Friday that Rob commented, "Keep that up at Kent tomorrow and you're gonna WIN IT." Well he shot a huge 867 overtaking the 2nd Place score to win by 35 pins and get that shirt a week later. Only a handful of times, over the last 24 years, has Ed put less than $20 in the Progressive Pot, surprisingly he only put in $10 Saturday. While still cashing a nice $1,933.00, he did leave $883.00 to carry over for next week's pot. CONGRATS ED for a long awaited return to the Winner's Circle!!

Excitedly we had a tie for 2nd & 3rd Place. Yanis Green and Johnny Seng both got locked in, shooting a strong 832 series. Yanis was sitting in 1st Place early, with what would often be a Winning score, with his hot streak also earning him an extra $330.00 from the Scratch Pot. Johnny shot a great 290 in his last game to pull out the tie with Yanis. Both great competitors walked away with $940.00 apiece.

Our next event will be at All-Star Lanes in Silverdale. This will be the first time the Tour will be bowling there with three squads at 11:00, 1:30, and 4:00. Hope to see everyone there... it's more fun to watch the game with a group!! GO SEAHAWKS!!!!
