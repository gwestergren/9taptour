BIG SURPRISE FOR 2nd TIME CHAMP
9-TAP JACKPOT REACHES $6,000

Great turnout this week at Bowlero Lanes (Lakewood), with 201 bowlers vying for the Title. Scores were right where we expected, with four bowlers tossing strong 800 series. Three of those 800's were bowlers chasing Title #2, while the fourth (Earl) was hoping for Title #14. It's always a good Tournament when everyone shooting 700 makes the 'Cash List'. Way to go all!!!!

Well, the 9-Tap Jackpot seems to be back to holding everyone at bay. Irene Balane & Charles Clarke sure gave it a scare though, both had the Front-5 earning themselves a share of the $500.00 Consolation Bonus. The Jackpot next week climbs to $6,297, so what does that mean? It means all consolation 'Ties' will be PAID IN FULL. Yep, that's right, no sharing starting next week.

What better phone call to get than someone telling you just earned Title #2. Chuck Marshall had headed home without knowing that he was the Champion. His second win came just short of the anniversary of his first Title! When Chuck opened in the 10th frame of his final game, he knew he would cash but thought he'd fallen short of the Title. Additionally, Chuck was not feeling well with a headache from a stiff neck, so he packed his gear and headed home. When Rob called him to give him the news, he was a bit surprised. Chuck finished with an awesome 840 series earning him $2,378.00 for the win. This seems to be Chuck's norm, he had opened in the 10th Frame at Hi-Joy in June 2025 and thought he'd fallen short of that Title too. He was surprised last year when his 841 also put him on top. As a thank you to Teddy Drake for her support, Chuck plans to give Teddy some "fun money" for her upcoming trip to Vegas to bowl the Women's National Tournament. Congratulations Chuck and good luck to Teddy in Vegas.

Also going for Title #2, shooting an impressive 832, and sitting on top most of the day, Yanis Green settled for 2nd Place earning himself a strong $1,061.00. A true bowler, even after shooting such an impressive score in squad 1, Yanis re-entered every squad to try to improve his score. I understand he won some Bracket money too.

Going for Title #14, Earl Kleist sure came close. Shooting his best set this year. His 813 earned a nice check for $526.00, which puts Earl's lifetime earnings on the Tour at $86,725.00. Wow, what an accomplishment.

Our 4th 800 series was Stephanie Lanouette, shooting 808 and going for Title #2 in less than a year as well! Congratulations to all our winners and we look forward to celebrating all of you on the Cash List in the near future.
